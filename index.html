Tactics & Rules

1. The Engine (3 Phases)

  • Phase 1 (Midfield): Ball possession. Based on Sp.Atk, Sp.Def and HP. Central midfielders dictate the play!
  • Phase 2 (Attack): Chance creation and finishing. Attackers and Wingers rule here. Strikers use ATK + Speed (Fast) or ATK + HP (Target Man).
  • Phase 3 (Defense): The wall. Defenders and CDM's use DEF, Sp.Def and HP to block and intercept.

2. Football Attributes

HP: Physicality, Target Man, Shielding.
ATK: Finishing, Shot Power, Penalties.
DEF: Tackling, Marking, Shot Stopping.
Sp.A: Technique, Vision, Passing.
Sp.D: Tactical Awareness, Interceptions.
SPE: Pace, Agility, Wing Play.

3. Robin Hood Chemistry

Chemistry boosts apply to the specific player with the links! Link adjacent players of the same Generation (+1) or Type (+1). Green lines = Massive stat boost!

The Robin Hood Rule: Weak players receive a MUCH larger boost from chemistry than overpowered Legendaries. Building a tactical team is better than drafting loose stars!

4. The 100% Tactical Pie

No more "weak link" penalties! Your team's score per phase is a clean weighted average based on the positions you chose. A speedy Winger perfectly contributes to the Attack score, but a Defender does not drag it down. Put the right players in the right positions!

5. Off-Season: Transfers & Training

  • Action Points (3 per season): Choose freely between training or transfers.
  • Transfers (1 AP): Swap out weak links for new scouts. Once started, you MUST choose a replacement!
  • Training (1 AP): Train a specific stat. Weak stats level up much faster (+3 to +6) than elite stats (+0 to +2).
