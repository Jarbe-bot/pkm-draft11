Tactics & Rules

1. The 3 Phases

  • Phase 1 (Midfield): Ball possession. Based on Sp.Atk, Sp.Def and HP (Strength).
  • Phase 2 (Attack): Chance creation and finishing. Attackers and Wingers rule here.
  • Phase 3 (Defense): The wall. Defenders and CDM's use DEF, Sp.Def and HP.

2. "Systeemvoetbal" Chemistry

Green lines = Type & Generation match (2 points). Yellow line = Type OR Gen match (1 point). Each point bridges 5% of the gap between a player's raw stat and the maximum (99). This means average players get massive boosts from teamwork, while 99-rated super-legendaries barely benefit. Build a balanced squad!

3. The 100% Tactical Pie

No more "weak link" penalties! Your team's score per phase is a clean weighted average based on the positions you chose. A speedy Winger perfectly contributes to the Attack score, but a Defender does not drag it down.

4. Training Camps

Between seasons, you earn 1 Training Session. Select a player and roll the dice to permanently upgrade a specific stat. The worse the stat is, the higher the dice roll (weak stats can gain +8, top-tier stats only gain +1 or +2). Develop your youngsters!
