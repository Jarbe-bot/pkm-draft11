Tactics & Rules

1. The Engine (3 Phases)

Your team's OVR doesn't matter during the match. The game calculates real football phases based on specific stats and hybrid roles:

  • Phase 1 (Possession): Midfielders (CM, DM, WM) control the game. High HP and Sp. Def win the ball.
  • Phase 2 (Creation): Wingers (WG), Attacking Mids (AM) and Wingbacks use Sp. Atk (Vision) and Speed to break lines.
  • Phase 3 (Finishing): Strikers (ST) need massive Attack (Finishing) & HP (Target Man) vs Defenders. AM's also help finish!

2. Football Attributes

HP: Physicality, Stamina, Target Man.
ATK: Finishing, Shot Power, Heading.
DEF: Tackling, Marking, Blocking.
Sp.A: Technique, Vision, Passing.
Sp.D: Tactical awareness, Interceptions.
SPE: Pace, Agility, Wing play.

3. Chemistry & Diversity

Link players of the same Generation or Type. Green lines (perfect match) give massive stat boosts!

🎁 Diversity Bonus

Playing with multiple generations active? You receive a free base Chemistry bonus (+2 points per extra generation) to compensate for the higher difficulty of finding matching links. The chaos is rewarded!
