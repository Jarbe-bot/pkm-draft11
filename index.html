How To Play

1. The Match Engine (3 Phases)

Matches aren't decided by raw OVR, but by a 3-phase simulation:

  • Phase 1 (Possession): Midfield controls the ball. High HP (Physicality) and Sp. Def (Tactics) win the midfield battle.
  • Phase 2 (Creation): Turning possession into chances. Your team's Sp. Atk (Vision/Passing) vs their Sp. Def.
  • Phase 3 (Finishing): Scoring the goal. Strikers use Attack (Finishing) & Speed vs Defenders' Defense & HP.

2. Football Attributes

HP: Physicality, Stamina, Hold-up play.
ATK: Finishing, Shot Power, Heading.
DEF: Tackling, Marking, Blocking.
Sp.A: Technique, Vision, Passing.
Sp.D: Tactical awareness, Interceptions.
SPE: Pace, Agility, Wing play.

3. Tactics & Chemistry

Don't just pick the highest OVR! A slow midfielder with high Sp.A and HP is a great playmaker. A fast winger with 0 HP can still outrun the defense.

Chemistry: Link players of the same Generation or Type to boost your team's overall performance. Green lines give the biggest boost!
